Privacy Policy

[APP NAME] · Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains how [LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY] (“we”, “us”), based in [COUNTRY], handles your personal data when you use [APP NAME], a mobile app for language exchange through voice calls. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email, or Apple/Google sign-in identifier; name, username, profile and language settings.
  • Calls: audio recordings of calls with other users and with our AI, transcripts of AI calls, and call metadata (time, duration, language, participants).
  • Tokens and purchases: token balance and history. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive your card details.
  • Device and usage: IP address, device model, OS, app version, advertising ID, crash and diagnostic data.
  • Support and reports: messages you send us and reports about other users.
  • Referrals: the referral code you use or share. We do not access your phone contacts.

2. Why we use it (legal bases)

  • Contract: to provide accounts, calls, tokens and AI feedback.
  • Consent: to record calls and to show personalized ads. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud and abuse prevention, moderation, and improving the app.
  • Legal obligation: tax, accounting and lawful requests from authorities.

3. Call recording

All calls, with humans and with AI, are recorded. We ask for your consent before your first call, and the app shows when a call is being recorded. Recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days. Access is restricted and happens only to handle a user report, for safety or abuse investigations, to resolve technical problems, or when required by law. If a report is open, we may keep the relevant recording longer while it is needed. We do not use voiceprints to identify you, and we do not use your recordings to train AI models.

4. AI processing

For AI calls, your audio and transcript are sent to our AI provider (currently Google) to generate the conversation and the end-of-call feedback. The feedback is automated and may contain errors. Our call infrastructure provider, LiveKit, may keep some anonymized session data longer than 30 days to improve its own technical models.

5. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the app:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage.
  • LiveKit: real-time voice call infrastructure.
  • Google: AI models, Google sign-in, AdMob ads, Google Play billing.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store billing.
  • Meta and other ad networks: advertising.

This list may change as we add or replace providers; we will update this page. We may also disclose data if required by law or in a merger or sale of the business.

6. Advertising

We show ads in exchange for free tokens. Ad partners may use your advertising ID and device data. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and EEA), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads; if you decline, you will see non-personalized ads. On iOS you can manage tracking in Settings. Under some US state laws, sharing data for advertising may count as “sharing” or “sale” of personal data; you can opt out at any time by contacting us or using the in-app privacy options.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. When required, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

8. Retention

  • Call recordings: 30 days (see section 3).
  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase and transaction records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Reports and moderation records: as long as needed for safety and legal claims.
  • Minimal ban record (see section 10): 1 year.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and other US state laws, Brazil’s LGPD, and other Latin American laws such as those of Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile and Peru), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, opt out of sale or sharing, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise them, use the in-app options or write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within the legal deadline, generally 30 days or less. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Account deletion

You can delete your account and data in the app (Settings). If you cannot access the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address linked to your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete or anonymize your data, including recordings, profile and transcripts, within a reasonable period (generally within 30 days), except records we must keep by law. Unused tokens are lost when the account is deleted.

If your account was closed for violating our Terms, you can still request deletion, but we keep a minimal record (a hashed identifier such as your email or sign-in ID, the date and the reason category) for 1 year to prevent repeat abuse and protect other users. We do not keep your recordings or profile for this purpose, except when a report or legal claim is still open. You may contact us to ask what data we hold or to appeal a decision.

11. Age

[APP NAME] is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that we have, we will delete it and close the account.

12. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and other reasonable measures. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Calls are with real people: avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. For material changes we will notify you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.

1. Datos que recopilamos

  • Cuenta: correo electrónico o identificador de inicio de sesión con Apple/Google; nombre, usuario, perfil y preferencias de idioma.
  • Llamadas: grabaciones de audio de llamadas con otros usuarios y con nuestra IA, transcripciones de las llamadas con IA y metadatos (hora, duración, idioma, participantes).
  • Tokens y compras: saldo e historial de tokens. Los pagos los gestionan Apple o Google; nunca recibimos los datos de tu tarjeta.
  • Dispositivo y uso: dirección IP, modelo de dispositivo, sistema operativo, versión de la app, identificador publicitario, datos de fallos y diagnóstico.

2. Para qué los usamos (bases legales)

  • Contrato: para ofrecer cuentas, llamadas, tokens y feedback de IA.

  • Obligación legal: obligaciones fiscales, contables y requerimientos legales de autoridades.

3. Grabación de llamadas

Todas las llamadas, con personas y con IA, se graban. Pedimos tu consentimiento antes de tu primera llamada y la app indica cuándo se está grabando. Las grabaciones se eliminan automáticamente a los 30 días. El acceso es restringido y solo ocurre para gestionar un reporte de usuario, investigar seguridad o abusos, resolver problemas técnicos o cuando la ley lo exija. Si hay un reporte abierto, podemos conservar la grabación pertinente más tiempo mientras sea necesaria. No usamos huellas de voz para identificarte y no usamos tus grabaciones para entrenar modelos de IA.

4. Procesamiento con IA

En las llamadas con IA, tu audio y su transcripción se envían a nuestro proveedor de IA (actualmente Google) para generar la conversación y el feedback final. El feedback es automático y puede contener errores. Nuestro proveedor de infraestructura de llamadas, LiveKit, puede conservar algunos datos de sesión anonimizados más de 30 días para mejorar sus propios modelos técnicos.

5. Proveedores de servicios

Solo compartimos datos con proveedores que nos ayudan a operar la app:

  • Supabase: base de datos, autenticación y almacenamiento de archivos.
  • LiveKit: infraestructura de llamadas de voz en tiempo real.
  • Google: modelos de IA, inicio de sesión con Google, anuncios AdMob, facturación de Google Play.
  • Apple: Iniciar sesión con Apple, facturación de la App Store.
  • Meta y otras redes publicitarias: publicidad.

Esta lista puede cambiar cuando añadamos o reemplacemos proveedores; actualizaremos esta página. También podemos divulgar datos si la ley lo exige o en una fusión o venta del negocio.

Mostramos anuncios a cambio de tokens gratuitos. Los socios publicitarios pueden usar tu identificador publicitario y datos del dispositivo. Cuando la ley lo exige (por ejemplo, en la UE, el Reino Unido y el EEE), pedimos tu consentimiento antes de mostrar anuncios personalizados; si lo rechazas, verás anuncios no personalizados. En iOS puedes gestionar el seguimiento en Ajustes. Según algunas leyes estatales de EE. UU., compartir datos con fines publicitarios puede considerarse “compartir” o “venta” de datos personales; puedes optar por no participar en cualquier momento escribiéndonos o usando las opciones de privacidad de la app.
